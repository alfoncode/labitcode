## The Problem

The rural land market in Spain remains opaque. Buyers, agents, and agricultural brokers rely on scattered documentation, in-person visits, and local knowledge to make decisions. Technical information — cadastral geometry, crop types, irrigation coefficients, slope data — is available through public services, but consolidating it requires hours of manual work.

## The Solution

Terreno Rústico digitizes the entire process. By entering a single cadastral reference, the platform automatically generates a **complete technical sheet** for any rural estate: geometry, agricultural enclosures, crop data, irrigation coefficients, altitude, and derived metrics. What used to take hours is now available in seconds.

## Who It's For

- **Landowners** looking to list their properties with verified technical data
- **Real estate agents** managing multi-owner portfolios
- **Agricultural brokers** intermediating crop operations
- **Buyers and investors** who need reliable data to evaluate opportunities
- **Industry professionals** seeking visibility in a geolocated directory

## Key Features

- **Automated Technical Sheets** — Cadastral and agronomic data consolidated from official government sources
- **Land Marketplace** — List, search, and manage rural estates
- **Harvest Marketplace** — Buy and sell crop productions linked to specific parcels
- **Professional Directory** — Geolocated profiles for agents, brokers, and agricultural technicians
- **Integrated Messaging** — Direct communication between buyers, sellers, and professionals
- **Admin Panel** — Content moderation and user management

## Visual Identity

A palette inspired by the Spanish countryside:

- **Olive green** — evoking olive groves and vineyards
- **Terracotta** — recalling Mediterranean clay soils
- **Serif typography** for headlines that conveys tradition and trust, paired with modern sans-serif for body text

## Tech Stack

Full-stack platform built with **TypeScript** end-to-end, modern frontend with **Next.js**, robust backend with **NestJS**, and geospatial database with **PostgreSQL + PostGIS** for cadastral geometry processing.
