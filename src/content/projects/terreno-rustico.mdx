## The Vision: Digitizing the Spanish Rural Real Estate Market

The market for rustic land, agricultural plots, and rural estates in Spain (*compraventa de terrenos rústicos, parcelas agrícolas y fincas rústicas en España*) has traditionally been opaque and fragmented. Buyers, farmers, real estate agents, and agricultural brokers have had to navigate multiple slow government databases, scattered local records, and manual paperwork just to verify basic information about a piece of land.

**Terreno Rústico** is the digital response to this complexity. It is a geospatial SaaS and marketplace built to simplify the buying, selling, leasing, and agronomic management of rustic properties in Spain. By entering a single cadastral reference (*referencia catastral*), the platform automatically consolidates public cadastral boundaries, soil usage, and crop data, compiling a complete, enriched technical sheet in seconds.

## Key Benefits for the Agricultural Ecosystem

Terreno Rústico is engineered to create value across the entire agricultural and rural transaction chain:

*   **For Landowners**: Instantly list your property with verified, government-backed technical data. No more guessing about soil admissibility, crop history, or slope. Accurate data attracts serious buyers and accelerates negotiations.
*   **For Real Estate Agents & Brokers**: Manage large portfolios of rural estates with dynamic cartographic files. Show clients exact property boundaries, crop codes, and irrigation coefficients directly from your browser.
*   **For Farmers & Agronomists**: Connect directly with landowners to rent agricultural plots (*alquiler de tierras de cultivo*) or buy/sell harvest yields directly at the source.
*   **For Investors**: Evaluate land productivity and development feasibility using structural metrics like altitude, average slope, and PostGIS compactness indexes.

## Core Features: Seamless Spatial Analytics

Terreno Rústico is not just a listing directory — it is a full-featured geographic information system (GIS) and transactional marketplace.

### 1. Automated GIS and Cadastral Sheets
By integrating with the official Spanish Catastro REST API and INSPIRE WFS, the platform automatically retrieves parcel boundary geometries. 

Since Catastro serves coordinates in local UTM projections (such as EPSG:25830), our NestJS backend uses the **PostGIS engine** to reproject coordinates to WGS84 (EPSG:4326) on the fly, rendering clean polygons on the map viewer.

### 2. Rich SIGPAC Enclosure Data
Every registered land is automatically checked against the agricultural **SIGPAC WFS service** to retrieve soil characteristics and enclosures:
- **SIGPAC soil usage** (e.g., olive trees, vineyard, pasture).
- **Average slope** and **altitude** (essential for machinery planning and subsidy evaluations).
- **Irrigation coefficients** and **admissibility indexes** (CAP / Coeficiente de Admisibilidad de Pastos).

### 3. Integrated Harvest Marketplace
A unique marketplace that connects harvest yields (crops, grains, olives, grapes) directly to the physical parcels they were grown on. This provides unprecedented traceability for agricultural buyers and food supply chains.

### 5. Geolocated Directory of Professionals
An interactive directory connecting landowners with local tractor drivers, drone operators, agronomic engineers, and land surveyors, sorted by proximity using PostGIS geographic distance queries.

## Tech Stack Overview

- **Frontend**: Next.js (App Router), React, TanStack Query, Auth.js, and **MapLibre GL JS** for fast vector tile rendering.
- **Backend API**: NestJS (built on Fastify) using Hexagonal Architecture and thin Domain Ports.
- **Database**: PostgreSQL with the **PostGIS extension** for spatial query optimization, caching, and spatial index matching (`ST_Intersects`, `ST_Centroid`).
- **ORM**: Drizzle ORM for type-safe database migrations and high-performance queries.
